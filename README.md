# Seba Parackal

I work across a mix of software, hardware, and creative projects.  
My interests range from low-level systems work to web development, 3D CAD for FDM printing, sound design, and experimenting with file formats and metadata.

## About
I’m a 4th year Computer Science student at Wilfrid Laurier University.  
Most of my projects come from coursework, personal experiments, or things I wanted to understand better.

## What I work on
- Systems-level programming and architecture experiments  
- Web development and tooling  
- 3D CAD focused on FDM additive manufacturing and printable design  
- Sound design and audio experimentation  
- Metadata workflows (media, files, and formats)
